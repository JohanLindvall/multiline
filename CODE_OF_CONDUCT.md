# Code of conduct

People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome in this project.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces.

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the code and the evidence, and offer
  specific, constructive feedback.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience. Assume good
  intent when a mistake can be corrected.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information,
  credentials, or correspondence without their permission.
- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized behavior,
  threats, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

If something goes wrong, you can contact the maintainer, Johan Lindvall,
privately at [johan.lindvall@gmail.com](mailto:johan.lindvall@gmail.com).
Include links and enough context to understand the situation. Reports will
be handled discreetly, with details shared only as needed to address them.

The maintainer may ask for a change in behavior, remove inappropriate
content, or limit participation temporarily or permanently according to the
severity and recurrence of the behavior. Reports and enforcement decisions
will be considered in context. Retaliation against someone who raises a
concern is unacceptable.

For a software vulnerability, use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
